The Retro & Gaming category is the largest in ThemeBulletin, with 22 themes that draw from the rich visual history of the early web, home computing, and arcade culture. These designs celebrate the personality-driven websites of the 1990s and early 2000s — handmade personal homepages, Neocities-style windowed layouts, Y2K browser chrome, vintage Windows 98 and XP desktops, and the bold pixel energy of arcade games. If you want a portfolio that immediately communicates character and nostalgia rather than corporate polish, this is the category to start with.

<Info>
  The Retro & Gaming category spans two boards in the gallery (22 themes, 16 per board). Use the **Next Board →** button to see all themes.
</Info>
