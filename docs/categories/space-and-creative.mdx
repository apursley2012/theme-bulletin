The Space & Creative category contains 11 portfolio themes that span a wide visual range, united by a shared emphasis on color, atmosphere, and expressive design. At one end of the spectrum you'll find deep-space observatory portfolios with starfield depth and scientific navigation; at the other, bright multicolor editorial layouts with large expressive typography and an approachable creative personality. In between are aurora gradient interfaces, retro-futuristic cosmic arcades, and textile-inspired handcrafted designs. This is the most visually diverse category in ThemeBulletin.

<Info>
  All 11 themes fit on a single board in the gallery. Each theme has its own live demo and standalone GitHub repository.
</Info>

## All 11 Themes

<Accordion title="Observatory">
  **Summary:** A cinematic observatory portfolio with starfield depth, scientific navigation, and a luminous central celestial focus.

  Observatory opens with the atmosphere of a working astronomical observatory — starfield depth, scientific navigation labels, and a luminous central focus that draws the eye like a celestial body. A striking choice for developers who want their portfolio to feel vast and carefully observed.

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/observatory](https://apursley2012.github.io/observatory/)
  - **Repository:** [github.com/apursley2012/observatory](https://github.com/apursley2012/observatory)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Space Mission">
  **Summary:** A mission-oriented space portfolio with orbiting elements, glowing planetary visuals, and a focused exploration narrative.

  Space Mission frames your portfolio as an active mission — orbiting interface elements, glowing planetary visuals, and a narrative that treats your career and projects as a focused journey of exploration. One of the more dynamic-feeling designs in the category.

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/space-mission](https://apursley2012.github.io/space-mission/)
  - **Repository:** [github.com/apursley2012/space-mission](https://github.com/apursley2012/space-mission)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cosmic Developer">
  **Summary:** A spacious cosmic portfolio that frames software development as a journey through systems, projects, and technical exploration.

  Cosmic Developer is the most developer-forward theme in the Space & Creative category, framing software development itself as a journey through systems and technical exploration. It occupies a natural middle ground between the Developer & Systems category and the broader cosmic aesthetic.

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/cosmic-developer](https://apursley2012.github.io/cosmic-developer/)
  - **Repository:** [github.com/apursley2012/cosmic-developer](https://github.com/apursley2012/cosmic-developer)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Aurora Cosmos">
  **Summary:** A calm celestial portfolio with layered aurora gradients, centered typography, and a polished deep-space atmosphere.

  Aurora Cosmos layers aurora gradients over a deep-space background to create a calm, polished celestial atmosphere. The centered typography and measured layout give it a professional, composed quality — spacious without feeling empty.

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/aurora-cosmos](https://apursley2012.github.io/aurora-cosmos/)
  - **Repository:** [github.com/apursley2012/aurora-cosmos](https://github.com/apursley2012/aurora-cosmos)

<Accordion title="Aurora Drift">

  Aurora Drift brings the gentle, drifting motion of the aurora to a restrained portfolio layout — cool luminous gradients, minimal navigation, and a smooth futuristic mood that feels effortlessly elegant rather than dramatic.

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/aurora-drift](https://apursley2012.github.io/aurora-drift/)

<Accordion title="Aurora Borealis">

  - **Live demo:** [apursley2012.github.io/aurora-borealis](https://apursley2012.github.io/aurora-borealis/)

<Accordion title="Colorful">

<Accordion title="Dyed in the Wool">
