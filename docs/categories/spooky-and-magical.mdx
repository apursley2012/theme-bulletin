The Spooky & Magical category contains 19 portfolio themes that embrace the dark, enchanted, and supernatural side of personal identity. These designs span a wide range within their shared aesthetic: at one end there are the witch-tech portfolios that merge terminal interfaces with spellwork geometry; at the other, haunted-house designs with moonlit scenery and playful ghost-story storytelling. In between you'll find digital alchemists, card-reading oracles, grimoire indexes, and nocturnal botanical portfolios. This is the category for developers who want a portfolio that is bold, atmospheric, and impossible to forget.

<Info>
  All 19 themes fit on a single board in the gallery. Each theme has its own live demo and standalone GitHub repository.
</Info>
